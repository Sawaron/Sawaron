### My part in DnD-Tenebres

The project was started by a friend. I joined as a co-developer, and my commits are [here](https://github.com/samurai3452345/DnD-Tenebres/commits?author=Sawaron). What I worked on:

- **Quest system:** quest templates and player quests (entities, status and type enums, repositories), a quest service, and a REST controller with endpoints to read quests
- **Event-driven progress:** events for killing a monster, looting an item and clearing a location, published from the combat service and used by the quest service
- **Loot:** monster loot table entries and a loot generator service
- **Database:** Liquibase migrations for new game content, including magic weapons and the quest tables
- **Game rules:** spell fields, effect types and targets, combat actions, and handling of stackable items
